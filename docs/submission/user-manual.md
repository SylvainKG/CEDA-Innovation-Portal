## 1. Everyone

**Register (applicants).** Choose **Register**. Enter your name, email address and a password of **at least 12 characters**, then accept the privacy notice. We email you a link: open it to verify your address, then sign in. Staff accounts are created by invitation from an administrator.

**Sign in.** Use your email address and password. If either is wrong, the message doesn't say which, for your protection. If you try too often, wait a few minutes and try again. In the demo, **Choose a demo portal** under the form fills in a demo account's email address; you then type the demo password you were given.

**Forgot your password.** Choose **Forgot password?** under the password field and enter your email. If an account exists, we send a single-use link that expires. The message is the same whether or not an account exists.

**Staff two-step sign-in.** Staff permissions require a second factor (an authenticator app); the server enforces this. The first time, the **Two-step sign-in** screen shows a QR code: scan it with an authenticator app (or type the key shown), then enter the 6-digit code. After that, enter a new code from the app when asked. Seeded demo staff accounts are exempt so that judges can sign in directly; the screen tells them so.

**Notifications.** The bell in the header shows how many updates are unread; **Notifications** lists them. The count refreshes every so often while you are signed in. **Email alerts** are optional: switch them on or off on the Notifications page **[confirm at release]**. In this demonstration they are **Simulated** (queued but not sent).

**Peolwane AI** **[confirm at release]**. The **Peolwane AI** button at the bottom right of every page opens an assistant for questions about using the portal. It starts in **Voice**: tap the microphone, speak, tap again, and the answer is shown and spoken. Choose **Text** to type instead. It only receives what you say or type in its panel, never your ideas or account, so don't share personal details with it. **Esc** closes it.

**Help.** **Help** holds the FAQs, regulatory contacts and how to report a problem. All times are Gaborone time (CAT).

**Staff home.** Staff accounts open on **Home**: counts for your role (for example ideas awaiting screening, reviews to do, chats waiting) and a list of what to do next. Each count and item links to the screen where you act.

**What you can never see.** Each person sees only what their role allows. The server refuses everything else, so a hidden button isn't the only protection.

## 2. Applicant

**Find a challenge.** Open **Challenges** to read a challenge brief: its purpose, deadline, evaluation criteria and any template to download.

**Start an idea.**
1. From the challenge or **My ideas**, choose **Start a new idea**.
2. Answer each section; **Continue** moves to the next. Your answers are **saved automatically** and also kept on your device, so a dropped connection doesn't lose your work. The indicator shows "Saved" or "Saved on this device".
3. **Attachments:** PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG or JPG, up to **10 MB each** and **10 files**. Wait until the upload finishes before you continue.
4. **Review & declare:** check your answers, accept the declaration and choose **Submit idea**.

**Your receipt.** You get a reference such as `CEDA-2026-0009`, the version and the time received. Keep the reference. If the screen says **"Not confirmed yet"**, your connection dropped; choose **Check status**. Submitting again is safe and can never create a second idea.

**Track your idea.** **My ideas** (it greets you by name) shows each idea's status (for example Submitted, Under review, Action required, Approved, Awarded) and a timeline of the five public stages: **Submission → Review → Feedback → Approval → Award**. The Feedback stage appears only if CEDA asked you something. "Action required" means CEDA is waiting for you, with a deadline.

**When CEDA asks you something.**
- **Clarification:** answer the question in a short reply. No new version is created.
- **Revision (Feedback Roadmap):** CEDA lists specific tasks. Choose **Revise your idea**, update your answers (replacing files in version 2 isn't available in this version), and for **each task** say whether you **Addressed** it, with a short explanation (up to 500 characters). Then choose **Review changes**: add a note on what changed, accept the declaration and submit **version 2**. Your reference stays the same, and version 1 is kept.
- After the deadline you can't reply until CEDA extends it.

**Outcome.** When CEDA publishes the decision you see **Approved** or **Not approved**, the reason and next steps. An **award**, if any, appears after approval.

**Live chat.** Talk to a CEDA consultant during service hours, **09:00–16:00 CAT on working days**. Outside those hours the chat says it is closed.

## 3. Screening officer

1. **Screening queue:** new submissions, oldest first.
2. Open a case and read the submission and files. Downloads use links that expire after 60 seconds, and each download is recorded.
3. **Record screening:**
   - **Eligible** moves the case to evaluation. Your reason stays with staff as an internal note, and the applicant sees only that the idea is under review.
   - **Ineligible** closes the case, and the applicant sees your reason.
4. **Request clarification** (a question and deadline) or **Request revision** (tasks and deadline) when needed.
5. **Internal notes** are visible to staff only, never to the applicant.

## 4. Reviewer

1. **My reviews** lists the cases assigned to you.
2. **Declare first.** Before the proposal opens, confirm you have **no conflict of interest** and will keep it confidential. If you have a conflict, declare it: your assignment is removed, the administrator reassigns the case, and the declaration is recorded.
3. **Score** each criterion from **1 to 5** using the anchored definitions, with a reason of **at least 20 characters**. Drafts are saved; **Submit** when complete.
4. You **can't see other reviewers' scores until you have submitted your own** on the same version.
5. **Version 2:** for a revised idea, verify each roadmap task as **Verified** or **Not verified**, with a note. **Compare versions** shows both versions side by side, with each change named. Version 1's scores stay with version 1.

## 5. Adjudicator

1. **Ready for decision** lists cases whose reviews have reached the quorum (two scorecards).
2. The **score panel** shows each reviewer's scores, the weighted total and the spread between reviewers.
3. **Record decision** (Approved or Not approved, with a reason). It stays a **draft, invisible to the applicant**, until you **Publish** it.
4. An award can only follow a published approval.

## 6. Administrator

The navigation has **All cases**, **Users & roles** and **Settings**. Settings opens **Challenges**; links at the top of every settings page lead to **Roles**, **Content** and **Chat settings**.

- **All cases:** every case with its stage; **assign reviewers** (at least two), see each reviewer's load, and remove a reviewer.
- **Record award** on an approved case (amount in BWP and a description), and **close** a case with a reason.
- **Challenges:** edit a challenge's details, publish, unpublish or close it to new ideas. Build its **application form** (sections and fields: short text, long text, choice, email), save it as a draft, then publish it. A submitted idea keeps the form version it used.
- **Rubrics:** add the criteria reviewers score, each with a weight and a description of what a 1, a 3 and a 5 look like. The weights must total 100%.
- **Content:** FAQs, contacts, referrals, the support page, the privacy notice, the declaration and other pages. Save as a draft or publish; only published pages are shown to the public.
- **Users & roles:** everyone registered, with their roles. Grant a role, remove one (with a reason), or **invite** staff by email (the email is Simulated in this demonstration). **You can't change your own roles:** the controls on your own row are disabled, and the server refuses and records the attempt anyway.
- **Roles:** what each role can do. Built-in roles keep their permissions; add a new role to combine permissions differently, and choose whether it needs two-step sign-in.
- **Chat settings:** whether chat is always open, always closed or follows the opening hours; the hours for each day of the week; and closure days (for example public holidays).
- **Other settings:** the review quorum (2 reviews) and the demo two-step exemption are configuration values with no screen; only the backend team can change them. Switch the exemption **off** for any real use.

Every change on these screens is recorded in the audit log.

## 7. Support consultant and support lead

- **Support inbox:** take a waiting chat, reply, and close it. One consultant can handle several chats.
- You **can't see proposals, files or scores**, by design.
- **Support lead:** chat statistics (volume, handled, missed, average wait) for a chosen period.

## 8. External committee member

**Home** shows how many cases are at each stage. **All cases** lists each case's **reference, challenge, stage and last update only**. You can't see proposal text, scores or internal notes (D-08).

## 9. Auditor

The **audit log** records every workflow action: who did it, what, when and the outcome. You can filter by case, action and date (a filter by person isn't available in this version). Entries can't be changed or deleted by any portal user, including administrators. The log holds no passwords, proposal text or note text.
